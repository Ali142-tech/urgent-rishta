Urgent Rishta is a matchmaking service. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, who can see it, and how you can have it changed or removed.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, password (stored in encrypted form), date of birth and gender.
  • Profile details: religion, community, country, city, marital status, height, education, profession, income range, family details, lifestyle and the partner preferences you choose. If you fill in a profile for a son, daughter, sibling or friend, we ask that you have their permission.
  • Photos: the photos you upload and, where we ask for it, a selfie to confirm that the profile is genuine.
  • Identity documents: only if we ask you for them as part of verification (for example an ID or proof of age). These are never shown to other members.
  • Messages and activity: interests you send and receive, and chat messages between members who have accepted each other's interest.
  • Technical data: your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit, used for security and to keep the site working.

We do not collect or store credit card details on this website. [CONFIRM: payments are made by bank transfer or another method outside the site. Correct this line if card payment is added.]

2. Who can see your profile

  • Visitors who are not logged in only see limited, blurred previews, never your contact details.
  • Registered members see the profile details you choose to show. Members can search profiles of the opposite gender only.
  • Your phone number and other contact details are shown only to members you have accepted (or who have accepted you), and as set in your privacy settings.
  • Our matchmakers and team can see profile details so that they can verify your profile and suggest suitable proposals.

3. Photos and consent

  • You decide whether your photos are visible, hidden or shared only after approval.
  • We only use your photo in public previews or on our website if you have agreed to it. Tell us at any time and we will remove it.
  • Do not upload a photo of another person without that person's permission.
  • We do not allow photos to be downloaded from the site, but we cannot stop someone from taking a screenshot. Please keep this in mind.

4. How we use your information

  • To create and show your profile and to suggest matches.
  • To verify profiles and keep members safe.
  • To send service emails and messages (verification, interests, matches). You can opt out of marketing emails at any time.
  • To improve the website and prevent misuse.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We share it only with: other members, as described above; our matchmaking team; service providers who help us run the site (for example email and hosting), who may only use it for that purpose; and authorities when the law requires it. Our trusted partners (see the Partners page) do not receive your personal information from us.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your profile while your account is active. 7. Updating or deleting your information

  • You can edit most of your profile yourself after logging in (My Profile).
  • To change details that are locked, such as your date of birth, or to hide, deactivate or delete your profile, contact us by WhatsApp, phone or email below. We may ask you to confirm who you are first.
  • You can ask to see the information we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted.

8. Security

We protect your information with encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls and password protection. No website can be completely risk-free. Please use a strong password, and be careful about the personal details you share with people you have not met. We advise families to verify the person and the intention before meeting.

9. Cookies

We use cookies to keep you logged in and to remember your settings. You can block cookies in your browser, but parts of the site may not work.
