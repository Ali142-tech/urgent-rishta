# Content drafts for Admin > Content

The Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy and FAQs are not in the code: they are saved in the database and edited in
**Admin > Content** (Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy, FAQs). These drafts replace the old text.

* Paste each draft into its Admin page (the HTML files can be pasted as they are; the FAQ file lists one question/answer per FAQ).
* Anything in **[CONFIRM: ...]** is a business or legal decision that only Urgent Rishta can make. Fill it in or delete the line
  before publishing.
* Have a lawyer read the Terms and the Privacy Policy before they go live (client point 14).
